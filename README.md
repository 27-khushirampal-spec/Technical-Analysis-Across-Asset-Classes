# Technical Analysis Across Asset Classes

## Project Overview

This project presents a comparative technical analysis of four financial market categories: commodities, cryptocurrencies, US stocks and currencies. The Excel workbook organises market observations across long-term, swing-trading and short-term trading horizons.

The analysis documents chart patterns, prevailing market trends and corresponding Buy/Sell recommendations for selected instruments.

## Objectives

- Examine technical chart patterns across multiple asset classes.
- Identify prevailing upward and downward market trends.
- Organise technical observations across different trading horizons.
- Interpret chart patterns to formulate directional trading views.
- Compare technical setups across commodities, crypto, US equities and currencies.

## Markets Covered

### 1. Commodities
Technical observations for selected commodity instruments, including chart patterns, trends and trading recommendations.

### 2. Cryptocurrencies
Technical analysis of selected cryptocurrency instruments across different trading horizons.

### 3. US Stocks
Chart-pattern and trend analysis of selected US-listed stocks.

### 4. Currencies
Technical observations of selected currency instruments and their market trends.

## Analytical Framework

The workbook includes:

- **Long-term analysis** — identification of broader chart patterns and market direction.
- **Swing trading analysis** — examination of intermediate-term trading setups.
- **Short-term analysis** — technical observations for shorter trading horizons.
- **Pattern recognition** — identification of formations such as double bottoms, head-and-shoulders patterns, triangles and price channels where recorded.
- **Trend assessment** — classification of prevailing market direction.
- **Trading signals** — Buy/Sell recommendations recorded in the workbook.

## Tools Used

**Microsoft Excel** — organisation of technical observations, chart-pattern analysis and consolidation of market recommendations.

## Project File

`TECHNICAL ANALYSIS_CATEGORIES(3).xlsx`

The workbook contains separate sheets for Commodities, Crypto, US Stocks, Currency and a consolidated Conclusion sheet.

## Scope and Limitations

This project documents technical analysis and trading recommendations. It should not be interpreted as a systematic indicator-backtesting study because the workbook does not establish a complete backtesting methodology, performance statistics or risk-adjusted returns.

The recorded recommendations represent observations within the workbook and do not guarantee future market performance.
